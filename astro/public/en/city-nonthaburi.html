Where to eat in Nonthaburi

Nonthaburi's signature food — real local spots, rounded up and ranked

Tod Man Nor Kala

A Koh Kret specialty — fish cakes made with nor kala, a local plant that's hard to find elsewhere

Mon sweets

Thai-Mon desserts on Koh Kret, from thong yip to khanom kong and other old-style sweets

Nonthaburi durian

Local Nonthaburi durian cultivars known for their flesh and their price — look for them in durian season

Boat noodles (kuay teow ruea)

Long-running boat-noodle shops are scattered across town, rich and full-flavored

Chao Phraya riverside dining

Restaurants and cafes along the river where you can eat and watch the boats pass at dusk

Tha Nam Nonthaburi street food

Grazing food around the pier and old market at local prices

Riverside cafes

Several river-view cafes around Koh Kret and along the Chao Phraya

About — Nonthaburi

Nonthaburi sits right on Bangkok's northern edge. Hop on the Purple Line train or the Chao Phraya Express Boat and you're there. Most people know it as a residential city, but there's plenty of riverside sights and old markets tucked away.

The highlight is Koh Kret, an island in the middle of the river with a Mon community, pottery, and street food you can graze on all day. It's perfect for a there-and-back day trip from Bangkok.

Best time: November–February, cool and comfortable — good for walking the riverside markets all day
