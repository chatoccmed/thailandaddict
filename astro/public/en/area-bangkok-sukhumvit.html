In short: The Asok–Nana stretch of Sukhumvit is the easiest base in Bangkok, sitting right where the BTS Asok and MRT Sukhumvit lines meet. Terminal 21 mall, food from every country, and the Soi 11 and Nana nightlife are all within walking distance. Great for first-timers and night owls. Rates run from about ฿800 to several thousand for the 5-star towers.
Why Sukhumvit

Sukhumvit — all in one place

The Asok–Nana part of Sukhumvit (Soi 1–23) sits right at the junction of the BTS Asok and MRT Sukhumvit lines, which is why so many travellers base themselves here. Malls, restaurants, and bars at every price point are all on your doorstep.

Things to do in Sukhumvit

Highlights and spots visitors love in this area

Terminal 21 Asok

Airport-themed mall at Asok station, each floor styled as a world city; a photo-stop foreign tourists pack daily

Benjakitti Forest Park

Green lung behind the convention centre with a lake, jogging tracks and an elevated skywalk locals run on

Queen Sirikit National Convention Center

Giant convention centre rebuilt in 2022 by the MRT, hosting book fairs, concerts and big expos all year

Kamthieng House Museum

A 170-year-old Lanna teak house at the Siam Society on Soi 21, a northern village amid the towers

Sukhumvit Street Market

Pavement stalls along Sukhumvit between Soi 4 and 11, evening till midnight, clothes, watches and souvenirs

Soi Cowboy

A 150-metre neon nightlife street between Soi 21 and 23, right across from Terminal 21

Nana Plaza (Soi 4)

Famous three-floor nightlife complex by Nana BTS, an easy walk and a fixture on the foreign-visitor map

Sukhumvit Soi 11

The clubs, rooftop bars and late-night eats strip near Nana that Thai and foreign night owls flock to

Soi 11 rooftop bars

Above Eleven and Pastel, the hit skyline rooftops on Soi 11 with good drinks, DJs and tables booked ahead

Sukhumvit Plaza (Korean Town)

Korean building at Soi 12 by Asok, full of BBQ joints, marts and karaoke, the area's real Korean enclave

SW1 Market (Soi 12)

Small night market at Soi 12 near Asok, afternoon till midnight, Thai street food after a mall run

Soi Arab (Soi 3)

Middle Eastern quarter off Sukhumvit, kebab smoke everywhere and late shisha cafes loved by Arab visitors

Where to eat in Sukhumvit

The dishes and spots this area is known for

Cabbages & Condoms

Quirky condom-themed Thai restaurant on Soi 12; proceeds fund public health and foreign diners love it

Pier 21 Food Court

Fifth-floor Terminal 21 food court, dishes 40-60 baht, every Thai region and queues of locals and tourists

Soi 11 street food

Pad thai, som tam, grilled seafood and mango sticky rice stalls open late on Soi 11 after a night out

Soi Arab kebabs & shawarma

Kebab, shawarma and falafel stalls on Soi 3/1, open late, full of flavour and friendly on the wallet

Sukhumvit Plaza Korean BBQ

Authentic Korean BBQ and stews in the Korean Town building at Soi 12, run by Korean chefs for Korean regulars

The Local

Michelin Thai restaurant in a blue heritage house on Soi 23, serving rare old family recipes

Baan Khanitha

Classic Thai restaurant in a Thai-house setting on Soi 23, known for curries and dips, great for guests

Soi 23 izakaya cluster

Dense run of Japanese izakaya on Soi 23, the real Japanese pocket of Asok, open late into the night
