Things to do in Buriram

Highlights and sights around Buriram — nature, city and culture

Phanom Rung

A Khmer stone temple atop Phanom Rung hill, the crater of an old volcano. The approach walkway and the temple itself are stunning, and it's the symbol of the province.

Chang Arena

Home ground of Buriram United. Match days are electric, with the whole town turning out in the club's Thunder Castle shirts to cheer.

Chang International Circuit

A proper international racetrack that holds major races every year. When there's an event on, the town fills up with fans.

Prasat Muang Tam

A Khmer temple not far from Phanom Rung, ringed by L-shaped ponds and naga balustrades. An easy stroll to pair with Phanom Rung.

Khao Kradong

An old volcano in the middle of town topped by a large Phra Suphat Bophit Buddha. Climb the naga staircase for views over the whole city.

Plearn Wan (Plern Plearn)

A large learning center and flower garden near Nang Rong district, with seasonal flower zones and enough activities to fill a day.

Where to eat in Buriram

Buriram's signature food — real local spots, rounded up and ranked

Isan food

Som tam, larb, koi, grilled chicken — bold, true Isan flavors you'll find in every district.

Goong jom

A specialty from Prakhon Chai district: fermented tiny shrimp with a sour-salty kick, eaten with fresh vegetables and pork belly.

Khao mao

Young rice pounded and tossed with coconut and sugar, a local snack found at markets and souvenir stalls.

Sen Chan pad pu & noodles

The town has several long-running noodle and stir-fried-noodle shops with friendly prices.

Mu kratha

DIY grill-and-hotpot joints are everywhere in town, a favorite dinner for Buriram locals, especially on match days.

Cafes and coffee

Plenty of new cafes have opened in town and around Chang Arena — a good spot to sip a coffee before heading out again.

Evening market street food

The night markets and walking streets in town serve grilled and fried snacks and sweets to graze on after dark.

Khanom jeen Prakhon Chai

Rice noodles with curry sauce and lower-Isan chili dip, soft strands piled with plenty of fresh vegetables.

About — Buriram

Buriram is a lower-Isan province that has reinvented itself in recent years, going from a quiet town to a sports city with both the Chang Arena football stadium and the Chang International Circuit, which hosts major races every year. At the same time the old side is still very much intact. Phanom Rung sits proudly on the crater of an extinct volcano and is one of the most beautiful Khmer temples in Thailand. Nearby you'll also find Prasat Muang Tam, more old volcanoes, and bold Isan food to try around the smaller towns. Buriram works for football fans, motor fans, and history buffs all in one trip.

Best time: November–February, cool and comfortable, ideal for walking the temples and being outdoors. In early April the sun aligns to shine straight through the doorways at Phanom Rung.
