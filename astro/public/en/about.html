A Thailand travel guide you can actually trust

ThailandAddict started with a problem travelers run into all the time — trustworthy reviews of hotels and destinations in Thailand are scattered, often incomplete, and the prices are rarely current. So we set out to gather, organize, and fact-check info on places to stay, see, and eat across Thailand in one place, to make planning your trip easier and more confident.

We do this for both Thai travelers who want to explore their own country and make every baht count, and international visitors heading to Thailand. Behind it all is a team that's worked in hotel reviews and online travel for over a decade — we know what travelers actually need from a review page: a location you can picture, prices you can really compare, and the downsides told straight, not just the praise.

A doctor who fell for travel

Behind ThailandAddict is one person — a doctor who turns days off into trips, and trips into stories that are genuinely fun to read.

📸 Doctor Chat on a trip in northern Thailand
Doctor Chat
Physician · Travel Writer · Founder of ThailandAddict

Behind ThailandAddict is Doctor Chat — a physician who loves travel and storytelling every bit as much as his work in medicine. Every time he packs his bag after a shift, he heads out to explore, taste, observe, and take notes — then turns it all into reviews that are easy to read and actually help people decide.

Over more than a decade, Doctor Chat has built travel websites under several brands across multiple countries, and ThailandAddict is the one he's dedicated entirely to Thailand — everything he knows about traveling here, gathered in one place.

The goal of ThailandAddict is simple — curate, organize, and rank places to stay, see, and eat across Thailand so you can plan a trip in minutes, without opening ten tabs at once ever again.

How we work

⚖️

Unbiased rankings

Hotels can't pay to move up the list or scrub the downsides. Rankings come from data and real reviews, nothing else.

💬

The good and the bad

Every review has a "Skip it if..." section that spells out exactly who a hotel is and isn't right for.

🎯

Quality over quantity

We'd rather build guides and reviews that are detailed and genuinely useful than crank out a lot of shallow ones.

What every review goes through

Every hotel review on ThailandAddict goes through the same steps, so the info stays accurate and fair.

1

Gather real guest reviews

We pull together hundreds of reviews from actual guests on Booking.com and Agoda.

2

Check location and transport

We check the distance to airports, stations, and key neighborhoods on real maps.

3

Compare prices across sites

We track the real price range across Agoda, Booking.com, and Trip.com.

4

Confirm it's still open

We check that the hotel is still open — not closed or renamed — and update its amenities to match reality before publishing.

5

Write both pros and cons

We sum it up in plain language and say clearly who it suits and who shouldn't book.
