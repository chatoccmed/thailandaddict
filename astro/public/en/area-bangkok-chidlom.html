In short: Chidlom–Ratchaprasong is Bangkok's luxury-shopping core, with CentralWorld, Gaysorn, Central Chidlom, and the Erawan Shrine all clustered together. Things to do in Chidlom–Ratchaprasong

Highlights and spots visitors love in this area

CentralWorld

Thailand's largest mall at the heart of Ratchaprasong, with 500-plus shops, a cinema, an ice rink and a plaza hosting year-round events.

Central Chidlom

The classic upscale department store dubbed The Store of Bangkok, strong on fashion and beauty, linked straight to Chidlom BTS station.

Gaysorn Village

Luxury boutique mall of world designer brands, with easy skywalk access and an upper floor of several notable restaurants.

Erawan Shrine

The famous four-faced Brahma shrine on the corner, a major tourist landmark where dancers perform thanks-offerings all day; free entry.

Trimurti Shrine

A shrine in front of CentralWorld believed to grant wishes for love, drawing crowds on Thursday nights with red roses and red incense.

Ganesha and Lakshmi shrines, Ratchaprasong

A cluster of Hindu deity shrines by CentralWorld and Gaysorn, Ganesha for work and arts and Lakshmi for fortune, all on one walk.

Amarin Plaza

A renovated long-standing Ratchaprasong mall linked by skywalk to CentralWorld, with eateries, gold shops and a Louis Vuitton flagship.

The Mercury Ville @ Chidlom

A community mall right at Chidlom BTS, packed with restaurants and cafes, a favourite meet-up and meal spot for the area's office crowd.

Big C Ratchadamri

The flagship Big C across from CentralWorld, great for cheap snacks and souvenirs with a busy food court, a tourist favourite for gifts.

Velaa Sindhorn Village Langsuan

An open-air lifestyle mall on Langsuan Road with restaurants, cafes and a premium supermarket, a leafy spot within walking distance of Lumphini Park.

Lumphini Park

A large central park, walkable from Chidlom via Langsuan, with a boating lake, jogging tracks and monitor lizards to spot at dawn and dusk.

Ratchaprasong Skywalk

An elevated walkway linking the whole district's malls from Chidlom to Ratchadamri BTS, letting you shop mall to mall without touching the street.

Where to eat in Chidlom–Ratchaprasong

The dishes and spots this area is known for

Paste Bangkok (Gaysorn Village)

Michelin-starred modern Thai by Chef Bee, refined bold flavours from old recipes in an elegant room with city views.

Gaggan (Gaysorn Amarin)

Chef Gaggan's world-renowned progressive Indian, an Asia's 50 Best fixture with limited seats that books out far ahead.

Red Sky (Centara Grand at CentralWorld)

A 55th-floor rooftop serving European cuisine and steaks with a 360-degree sky bar, ideal for a special evening.

Menya Itto (Gaysorn Village)

A famous Tokyo ramen brand best known for tsukemen with homemade noodles and a rich fish broth; lunchtime queues run long.

Erawan Tea Room (Grand Hyatt Erawan)

A classic tea room serving authentic Thai dishes and Thai-style afternoon tea in a retro setting, popular for lunch and snacks.

Ratchaprasong street stalls (outside CentralWorld)

Popular roadside stalls for grilled fish, noodles and cheap one-plate Thai meals, open evening to late near the bus stops.

CentralWorld dining floor (7th floor)

A whole floor of dozens of restaurant brands, Thai, Japanese, Korean and dessert, all air-conditioned and easy for groups.

Langsuan dining (Velaa Sindhorn Village)

A relaxed strip of restaurants and cafes on Langsuan Road, leafy and easygoing, with Thai, Western and chill-out spots near Lumphini Park.
