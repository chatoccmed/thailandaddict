Things to do in Khon Kaen

Highlights and sights around Khon Kaen — nature, city and culture

Bueng Kaen Nakhon

A lake in the middle of town that doubles as the city's park. In the evening people come to jog, cycle, and sit eating by the water.

Phu Wiang Dinosaur Museum

A real dinosaur fossil dig site in Wiang Kao district, with skeletons and models that get kids excited.

Wat Nong Waeng (Phra Mahathat Kaen Nakhon)

A nine-tier pagoda beside Bueng Kaen Nakhon — climb to the top floor for a wide view over the city and the lake.

Mudmee silk and the silk festival

Khon Kaen is known for mudmee silk, with weaving villages and a big annual silk festival late in the year.

Khao Niao Road

The nightlife strip in the city center, full of restaurants and bars — and the liveliest Songkran water-fight spot in town.

Ubol Ratana Dam

A large dam on a wide stretch of water, with floating riverside restaurants and a sunset viewpoint where people settle in for a while.

Where to eat in Khon Kaen

Khon Kaen's signature food — real local spots, rounded up and ranked

Isaan food

Som tam, larb, koi, nam tok, grilled chicken — you'll find them in every corner of town, bold and full-on the way real Isaan food should be.

Grilled chicken and som tam by the lake

Grilled chicken and som tam stalls line the edge of Bueng Kaen Nakhon — easy to sit and eat in the cool breeze by the water.

Mam and Isaan sausage

Tangy fermented meats eaten with sticky rice and fresh vegetables — both a snack and a classic local souvenir.

Sticky rice and grilled snacks on Khao Niao Road

A nightlife strip packed with grilled and fried snacks plus bars to sit at — good for a long evening of grazing.

Moo kratha and jim jum

Moo kratha grills and jim jum hot pots are everywhere in town — a go-to dinner for locals in Khon Kaen.

Cafes and coffee in town

Khon Kaen has plenty of cafes both downtown and around the university — fine for working or taking photos all day.

Khanom jeen and Vietnamese kuay jap

Khanom jeen with curry sauce and the wide-noodle Vietnamese kuay jap, found at morning markets and old-school shops.

About — Khon Kaen

Khon Kaen is a big city in the heart of Isaan that people are always passing through. Bueng Kaen Nakhon, a lake right in the middle of town, is where locals jog and sit out for the evening breeze. The Phu Wiang Dinosaur Museum sits on a site where real fossils were dug up nearby. There are villages that weave mudmee silk out in the countryside, plus a big annual silk festival. And around town you'll find cafes and Isaan restaurants where you can sit all day. If you want an Isaan trip in a city that has everything sorted, Khon Kaen works.

Best time: November–February, cool and comfortable — lines up with the silk festival and the city's seasonal events
