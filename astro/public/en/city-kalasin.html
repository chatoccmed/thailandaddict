Things to do in Kalasin

Highlights and sights around Kalasin — nature, city and culture

Phu Kum Khao dinosaurs

Kalasin is Isan's dinosaur town. In Sahatsakhan district you'll find the fossil excavation pit and the Sirindhorn Museum, which keeps real skeletons on display.

Praewa silk

The Praewa-pattern silk of the Phu Thai people around Kham Muang district is the province's signature — fine, hand-woven, and a craft still very much alive.

Lam Pao Dam

A large earthen dam near town where Kalasin locals come to catch the breeze and eat fish by the water, with Dok Ket beach for a swim in the hot season.

Phu Phan range

The mountains across the south of the province, full of waterfalls, forest, and viewpoints — a stretch of nature that runs through several districts.

Phu Thai culture

Kham Muang and Kuchinarai districts are Phu Thai country, still keeping their language, dress, and traditional weaving.

Phra Phrom Phumipalo

A large Buddha image standing in the middle of a reservoir at Sahatsakhan, clearly visible from the shore — a natural pairing with a Phu Kum Khao visit.

Where to eat in Kalasin

Kalasin's signature food — real local spots, rounded up and ranked

Isan food

Som tam, larb, koi, grilled chicken, all eaten with sticky rice — found all over town, with the bold, real-deal Isan punch.

Lam Pao Dam freshwater fish

Restaurants along Lam Pao Dam serve fresh fish — grilled, tom yum, and fish larb — eaten by the water in the cool breeze.

Isan sausage and mam

Sour fermented meats grilled and eaten with fresh vegetables and sticky rice — a snack and a local souvenir.

Pla ra and jaew bong

Pla ra (fermented fish) and jaew bong chili dip are kitchen staples in Kalasin, easy to find at fresh markets — hot and eaten with raw vegetables.

Mu krata and jim jum

Mu krata grill spots and jim jum hot pots are all over town — a favorite dinner for workers and families.

Phu Thai food

Around Kham Muang and Kuchinarai you'll find Phu Thai home cooking like chicken soup with vegetables, plus local ferments with their own distinct taste.

Cafes and coffee

The town has a number of new cafes — an easy place to sit with a coffee mid-day before heading back out.

Morning markets and street food

Markets in town have grilled pork with sticky rice, khanom chin, and grilled snacks to graze on from morning till evening.

About — Kalasin

Kalasin is a quiet Isan town with more to see than you'd expect. This is one of Thailand's richest dinosaur fossil sites — the Sirindhorn Museum and the Phu Kum Khao excavation pit let you walk up and look at real bones. The south of the province runs into the Phu Phan range, with waterfalls and viewpoints, while the Kham Muang and Kuchinarai districts are Phu Thai country, home to the hand-woven Praewa silk the province is known for. Around the town, Lam Pao Dam draws people for fish by the water. If you like easygoing Isan trips with real nature, history, and craft, Kalasin is worth a stop.
