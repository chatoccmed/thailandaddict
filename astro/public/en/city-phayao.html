Things to do in Phayao

Highlights and sights around Phayao — nature, city and culture

Kwan Phayao

The freshwater lake in the heart of town that defines Phayao. Every evening you can sit by the water and watch the sun set behind the hills.

Wat Tilok Aram

A temple in the middle of Kwan Phayao. Take a boat out to pay respects at the shrine in the lake — a must-stop for anyone visiting Phayao.

Wat Sri Khom Kham

The town's principal temple, home to Phra Chao Ton Luang, the largest Buddha image in the city, sitting right by the lake.

Doi Phu Langka

A well-known mist viewpoint in Pong district. Hike up and camp overnight to catch the fog in the morning.

Phu Sang & Chiang Kham district

The eastern side of the province has waterfalls, a national park, and Tai Lue culture for a taste of local life.

Phayao Walking Street

Open on weekends along the lake, with local food, craft stalls, and an easygoing small-town feel.

Where to eat in Phayao

Phayao's signature food — real local spots, rounded up and ranked

Northern Thai food

Khao soi, nam ngiao, and gaeng hang lay are everywhere in town, cooked the way northerners really make it.

Freshwater fish from the lake

Kwan Phayao is a source of freshwater fish — lakeside spots serve grilled fish, fried fish, and tom yum with fresh catch.

Sai ua & crispy pork rind

Classic northern souvenirs and side dishes. Eat them with sticky rice or take a pack home.

Khanom jeen nam ngiao

Rice noodles in nam ngiao are a regular breakfast for locals — a tomato-based broth with dried red kapok flowers.

Tai Lue food

Around Chiang Kham there are Tai Lue communities with their own local dishes and distinctive chili dips to try.

Lakeside cafes

Coffee shops with lake views keep opening up — a good spot to sip and watch the water for a while.

Phayao morning market

The fresh market in town has local vegetables, northern snacks, and traditional sweets to graze on.

About — Phayao

Phayao is a quiet northern town built around Kwan Phayao, a wide freshwater lake right in the middle of the city. In the evening people gather along the water to sit, cycle, and wait for the sun to drop behind the hills. Around town there are old temples, mountains you can climb for a sea of morning mist, and small cafes that keep popping up one by one. If you like places that are in no hurry and easy to settle into, Phayao fits.

Best time: November–February, cool and clear — comfortable for sitting by the lake
