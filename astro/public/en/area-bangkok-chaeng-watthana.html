Things to do in Chaeng Watthana

Highlights and spots visitors love in this area

Chaeng Watthana Government Complex

The area's huge cluster of government offices, including consular, court and dozens of agencies, now reached by the Pink Line

Central Chaengwattana

The biggest mall on the north side with over 300 shops, SF Cinema, bowling and a food court, a key local hub

IMPACT Muang Thong Thani

One of Asia's largest expo and concert venues nearby, hosting big trade fairs and major concerts year round

Cosmo Bazaar Muang Thong Thani

A lifestyle mall in central Muang Thong with over 30 restaurants, a food hall and supermarket, a hangout around IMPACT events

IT Square Laksi

A long-standing mall at Lak Si circle with Foodland, a cinema, food court and the Immigration Division 1 office

Software Park Thailand

An IT and software developer hub on Chaeng Watthana Road under NSTDA, making this a tech employment district

Wat Lak Si

A royal temple and the only old temple in Lak Si district, with a relic chedi and a revered shrine, popular for merit-making

Khlong Prem Prachakon

A historic canal dug under King Rama V that gave Lak Si its name from the fourth marker post, running along Vibhavadi Road

Lak Si Interchange Station

A key transit interchange where the Red Line links to the Pink Line, making trips into town and to Don Mueang easy

Don Mueang Airport

Don Mueang Airport sits only about 7 kilometres away, a few minutes by Red Line commuter train from Lak Si

Where to eat in Chaeng Watthana

The dishes and spots this area is known for

Government Complex Food Market

A market-style hall with over a hundred stalls, full Thai dishes at 50 to 80 baht, busiest at the lunch break

Hua Seng Hong Central Chaengwattana

A famous Chinese restaurant in the mall, known for crispy pork, roast duck and seafood hotpot for family meals

Food Patio Central Chaengwattana

The ground-floor food court mixing Thai, Japanese, Korean, sushi, burgers and crepes with easy card payment

Cosmo Bazaar Food Hall

The food hall and food court inside Cosmo Bazaar gathering Katsuya, Yayoi, MK and S&P under one roof

Foodland IT Square

A long-hours supermarket with an open kitchen at Lak Si circle, selling fresh groceries and cooked food at fair prices

Boonjaroen Night Market

Muang Thong Thani's evening market with rows of street food stalls at local prices, great for an after-work stroll

Isan @ Arena IMPACT

An Isan restaurant at Muang Thong with bold som tam, grilled chicken and larb, handy before or after a concert

Cosmo Walk Muang Thong Thani

A semi-open dining strip near Cosmo Bazaar with cafes, seafood spots and easygoing evening bars for the local crowd
