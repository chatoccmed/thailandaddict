Things to do in Koh Larn

Highlights and sights around Koh Larn — nature, city and culture

Tawaen Beach

The main and busiest beach on Koh Larn, with white sand, clear water, the full range of water sports, restaurants, and deck chairs for rent. This is where the big boats from Pattaya dock.

Samae Beach

A white-sand beach with clear water and a quieter, more natural feel than Tawaen. Good for relaxing and snorkeling over the coral just off the beach.

Nuan Beach (Monkey Beach)

A small, peaceful beach with very clear water, good for escaping the crowds. The mood is private, with a few small restaurants along the sand.

Tien Beach

A beach on the other side of the island, calm with clear water, water sports, and beachside restaurants. Good if you want to avoid the crowds at Tawaen.

Water Sports

Koh Larn has the full set — parasailing, banana boats, jet skis, and snorkeling — especially at Tawaen Beach. It's one of the island's most popular activities.

Koh Larn Viewpoint

A viewpoint on a hill in the middle of the island, looking out over the beaches and clear water all around, with a Koh Larn sign. A popular photo spot.

Where to eat in Koh Larn

Koh Larn's signature food — real local spots, rounded up and ranked

Beachside Seafood

Tawaen Beach and the pier at Na Ban have restaurants with fresh seafood — prawns, shellfish, crab, and fish, grilled, steamed, or stir-fried, eaten by the sea in the breeze. Prices depend on the place and the season.

Beach Restaurants and Deck Chairs

Along the beaches there are made-to-order food and drink spots with deck chairs for rent, so you can eat and swim all day. An easy, relaxed beach vibe.

Cafes and Snacks

The Na Ban pier area and Tawaen Beach have cafes, dessert shops, and snacks to drop by — while you wait for the boat or take a break from swimming.

Local Island Food

In Na Ban village there are made-to-order shops and rice-and-curry spots at local prices, a cheaper option than the beachside restaurants for those staying overnight.

About — Koh Larn

Koh Larn is a small island just off Pattaya in Chonburi province, reachable by boat in under an hour. It's a clear-water getaway that people from Bangkok and visitors come to for a day trip or an overnight, because the sand is whiter and the water far clearer than the beaches on the Pattaya side.

The island has several beaches to choose from. Tawaen is the main, busiest beach, with the full range of water sports — parasailing, banana boats, and snorkeling. Samae and Nuan are quieter with clear water and are good for lazing around, while Tien is a calmer beach on the other side. If you want to stay over, there are places to sleep and seafood restaurants on the island. Koh Larn works for swimmers, water-sports fans, and anyone who just wants to relax by the beach. Honesty: it gets very crowded on holidays and the boats are packed, so go early; check the price and safety of water sports before you ride; the sea is murky on some days depending on the weather; and help keep the beaches clean by taking your trash back with you.

Best time: November–April brings good weather and clear water, ideal for swimming, snorkeling, and water sports — this is the popular season. The rainy season, May–October, has spells of rain and murky water on some days, but you can still get there by boat.
