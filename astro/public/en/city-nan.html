Things to do in Nan

Highlights and sights around Nan — nature, city and culture

Wat Phumin

A cruciform temple in the heart of town with the Pu Man Ya Man mural, the famous whispering lovers, the image most people picture when they think of Nan

Kad Khuang Mueang Nan Walking Street

Opens in the evening in front of Wat Phumin, with local food, khantoke meals, and an old-town atmosphere for a long stroll

Pua District

A small town amid the rice fields with the Tai Lue way of life, coffee shops over the paddies, Tai Lue temples, and the road up Doi Phu Kha

Bo Kluea

A village that still boils salt from mountain rock-salt wells the old way, a craft unique to Nan

Doi Samer Dao - Pha Chu, Sri Nan

A camping spot to watch the sea of mist and the sunrise over the Nan River, with wide-open views morning and evening

Sky Road 1256, Pua - Bo Kluea

A drive along the ridgeline with layers of mountains on both sides, the highlight of any Nan road trip

Where to eat in Nan

Nan's signature food — real local spots, rounded up and ranked

Northern Thai food

Khao soi, nam ngiao, kaeng hang le, and northern larb are easy to find all over town, bold flavors the way northerners like it

Nan khao soi

Several long-running khao soi shops in town, soft noodles in a rich curry broth, eaten with pickled greens and shallots

Sai ua, kaep mu, nam phrik

Northern dishes to go with sticky rice, and you can buy them to take home too

Tai Lue food

Around Pua and Thung Chang there are Tai Lue communities with their own nam phrik and local vegetable dishes

Nan cafes and coffee

Nan grows arabica coffee up in the hills, and both in town and in Pua plenty of cafes look out over the paddies and mountains

Ma fai chin drinks

Ma fai chin (wampee) is a fruit Nan is known for, sold fresh, as juice, and made into souvenirs

Morning market - Nan fresh market

The morning market has sticky rice, nam phrik, local vegetables, and regional sweets to graze on

Local sweets

Khao kaep, khao khuap, and nam ngiao noodles are breakfast staples that people in Nan eat regularly

About — Nan

Nan is a northern town in a valley that you can wander around all day without getting bored. In town there are old temples like Wat Phumin, with its famous Pu Man Ya Man mural worth a stop, and small streets packed with cafes and northern Thai restaurants. Outside town, the roads climb the mountains toward Pua and Bo Kluea, passing terraced rice fields, seas of mist, and the still-living Tai Lue way of life. If you like slow towns and easy road trips, Nan is a province you can settle into for several unhurried days.
