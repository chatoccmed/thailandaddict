📝 Editorial Policy

How We Write

ThailandAddict sticks to one standard: reviews that are transparent, fair, and honest with the reader. Here's exactly how we approach every single one.

How We Score

Every hotel is rated across 6 dimensions out of 10, drawing on real guest reviews and details our team checks for ourselves:

  • Location — close to public transport and the heart of town
  • Cleanliness — the standard of the rooms and service
  • Service — staff and how willing they are to help
  • Value — the price against what you actually get
  • Amenities — Wi-Fi, breakfast, and the rest
  • Overall — how the whole stay feels

Affiliate Disclosure

ThailandAddict is an affiliate partner of Agoda, Booking.com, Trip.com and Klook. When you click a link and book through us, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Important: The commission we earn has no bearing on what we write. We rank and score on real quality, not on commission rates.

Corrections

Hotels and destinations change all the time. If you spot anything that doesn't match reality, let us know and we'll update it right away.

Our Review Process

Every review is checked by the editorial team before it goes live, and again within 30 days of a stay, so the information stays fresh and accurate.
