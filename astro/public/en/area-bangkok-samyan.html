Things to do in Sam Yan–Chula

Highlights and spots visitors love in this area

Samyan Mitrtown

A mixed-use complex opened in 2019 with a 24-hour zone, eateries, a cinema and a never-closing bookstore, linked to MRT Sam Yan

Chulalongkorn University campus

Thailand's oldest university, founded in 1917, with pink Thai-revival faculty buildings and leafy grounds made for wandering

Chula art and architecture landmarks

The Chula Memorial Hall, Maha Chulalongkorn building and the Thai-style shrine in the heart of campus draw architecture photographers

Chulalongkorn Centenary Park

A green flood-resilient park opened in 2017 with a sloping lawn, walking paths and water bikes, a short stroll from Samyan

Samyan Market

Chula's fresh market, now on Soi Chula 32, selling produce, seafood and flowers, with a budget food court upstairs

Banthat Thong food street

A buzzing street-food road beside Chula, lively from dusk till late, lined with dozens of famous spots students and tourists flock to

Wat Hua Lamphong

A temple from King Rama IV's era on Rama IV Road, well known for its coffin-donation merit-making tradition

Chamchuri Square

An office and retail tower linked straight to MRT Sam Yan, gathering restaurants, cafes and clinics

Suan Luang Square

A Chula community plaza near Uthen Thawai that turns into an evening flea market of food stalls and craft shops full of student buzz

Queen Saovabha Snake Farm

The Thai Red Cross snake farm, walkable from Samyan, with venom-milking shows and an educational reptile museum

Lido Connect

The former Lido cinema in Siam Square, reborn as an events, live-music and theatre space close to the Samyan area

Block 28 evening market

An event ground by the Centenary Park hosting flea markets, craft fairs and outdoor activities with a relaxed evening vibe

Where to eat in Sam Yan–Chula

The dishes and spots this area is known for

Banthat Thong food street

A buzzing street-food road by Chula, lively from dusk till late, packed with noodle, suki and dessert spots

Jeh O Chula

A legendary late-night spot off Banthat Thong, famous for giant tom yum mama noodles loaded with toppings

Somboon Seafood

A long-running Chinese-style seafood house born beside Chula's accounting faculty, famed for its curry crab

Banthat Thong roast duck

Several Chinese-style roast-duck and noodle shops along Banthat Thong, with tender duck and rich gravy and long evening queues

Samyan Mitrtown food court

A late-opening dining zone gathering Samyan and Saphan Lueang favourites, many cuisines at student prices

Hom Duan khao soi

A northern Thai eatery inside Samyan Mitrtown, loved for rich chicken khao soi and pick-your-own curry plates

Joke Sam Yan

Smooth pork rice porridge with a soft egg and fried dough, a long-standing morning and late-night staple here

Suan Luang Square food stalls

Food stalls and casual sit-down spots near Uthen Thawai, gathering street snacks, fried bites and friendly-priced drinks at dusk
